Fair questions

Is my data really private?

Yes — structurally, not just by promise. TipBud has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your shifts live on your phone and, if you turn sync on, in your own private iCloud, which is tied to your Apple Account and technically inaccessible to us.

What happens after my 30 free shifts?

Logging new shifts asks for the one-time unlock. Everything you already logged stays fully viewable and exportable, paid or not — your history is never held hostage.

I already have years of tips in another app.

Bring them. Export a CSV from your old tracker and use Import CSV in TipBud — columns are matched automatically, and imported shifts never count against your free 30.

New phone — do I pay again?

No. The unlock is tied to your Apple Account: tap Restore Purchases on the new phone and you're in. Your history follows via iCloud or a backup file.
