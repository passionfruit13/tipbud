# TipBud Privacy Policy

*Effective: July 2026*

TipBud is built so that we **cannot** see your data.

## What we collect

Nothing. TipBud has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no servers of ours. We never see your shifts, tips, jobs, or anything else you enter.

## Where your data lives

- **On your device.** Everything you log is stored locally on your iPhone.
- **Your private iCloud (optional).** If iCloud Sync is on, your data syncs through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It is encrypted in transit, tied to your Apple Account, and technically inaccessible to us. You can turn this off in Settings → Backup & sync.
- **Backup files you create.** CSV exports and backup files are created on your device and go only where you send them.

## Purchases

The optional lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

## Notifications

If you enable a shift reminder, the notification is scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Data deletion

Delete the app and your local data is gone. To also remove synced data, delete TipBud's data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
