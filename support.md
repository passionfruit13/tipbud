# TipBud Support

Thanks for using TipBud — the tip tracker that's $14.99 once, with a history that survives anything.

## Common questions

**How do I log a shift?**
Shifts tab → **Log Shift**. Pick the job, enter hours (or start/end times), cash tips, card tips, sales — tip-outs are computed from your rules automatically. About 20 seconds once you're used to it.

**How is my hourly rate calculated?**
(Hourly wage × hours + tips − tip-outs) ÷ hours. It's your *true* rate — after tip-outs, including wages.

**How do I set up tip-out rules?**
Settings → Jobs & Wages → your job → **Add Tip-Out Rule** (e.g. Busser, 2% of sales). Every new shift computes tip-outs from your rules; you can always override a single shift.

**How do I move my history from another app?**
Export a CSV from your old app, save it to Files, then in TipBud: Settings → **Import CSV**. Columns are matched automatically and you can adjust the mapping before importing. Imported shifts never use up your free-shift trial.

**Is my data backed up?**
Two ways, both free: private iCloud sync (Settings → Backup & sync — no account needed, invisible to us) and manual backup files (Settings → **Back Up to File…**), which you can restore on any device.

**What's free and what's paid?**
Your first 30 hand-logged shifts include every feature. After that, a single $14.99 purchase unlocks unlimited shifts forever. No subscription, no ads. Your history stays viewable and exportable even if you never pay.

**I bought it on my old phone — how do I unlock my new one?**
Settings → **Restore Purchases** (same Apple Account). Free, always.

**The currency symbol is wrong.**
Settings → **Currency** — pick your currency. It changes display only; your numbers are never converted.
